InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization dedicated to creating sustainable social impact across India. Founded on September 23, 2020, by Govind Shukla and headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, the foundation works at the intersection of community relief, education, women empowerment, environmental protection, and youth skill development. Driven by a passion for social change and supported by a nationwide network of volunteers and interns, we deliver ground-level solutions to uplift underserved communities.

About Our Work

Our core initiatives—Project SEVA (hunger & relief), Project BACHPANSHALA (child education), Project UDAAN (women's empowerment), Project JEEV (animal welfare), Project PRAKRITI (environmental sustainability), and Project VIKAS (youth internship & skill training)—are designed to build an inclusive and self-reliant society. Recognizing our commitment to transparency and operational excellence, InAmigos Foundation is registered with NITI Aayog (NGO Darpan), ISO 9001:2015 certified, and authorized for 12A, 80G, and CSR-1 partnerships.
